# Best 11 Principles

## Attacking
- Prefer controlled low-driven finishes over maximum-power shots when the angle is tight.
- Use a quick shoot-then-pass fake to change direction and disrupt a defender.
- Lead wide runners into space with a charged through ball.
- Cut inside from the wing and target the far post with a finesse finish.
- Shape free kicks around the wall; vary power and curve.
- Mix front-post and far-post corner deliveries.
- Rotate wingers and central midfielders near the 60th minute when stamina falls.

## Defending
- Do not hold PRESS constantly; protect central lanes with a defensive midfielder.
- Prefer jockeying and standing tackles; slide only when the risk is justified.
- Rush the goalkeeper selectively in one-on-one situations.
- Use a Deep or Normal defensive line when opponents threaten with balls over the top.
- Keep Balanced mentality by default; protect a narrow late lead with a more defensive setup.
