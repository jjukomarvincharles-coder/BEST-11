# Tactics

Formation presets: 4-3-3 Attack, 4-2-3-1 Wide, 4-1-2-1-2 Diamond and 3-5-2.

Mentality: Balanced, Attacking, Ultra-Defensive.
Sliders: defensive line (Deep/Normal/High), press intensity and attack speed.
Roles: Inverted Fullback, Mezzala, False 9 and Classic Winger.
Pre-match board milestone: drag players between formation slots, manage substitutes and assign corner/free-kick/penalty takers.
